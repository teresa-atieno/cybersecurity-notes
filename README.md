## Lesson 1: What is Cyber Security?

Cyber Security is the practice of protecting computers, networks, and data from attacks, damage, or unauthorized access.

### The CIA Triad - The 3 Most Important Rules

1. **Confidentiality** - Only the right people can see the data. Example: Passwords keep your account private.
2. **Integrity** - Data must not be changed by hackers. Example: Nobody can change your exam marks.
3. **Availability** - Data must be available when you need it. Example: Your school system should not go down.

### Common Types of Attacks

- **Malware** - Bad software like viruses
- **Phishing** - Fake emails that trick you to give your password
- **Password Attacks** - Guessing or stealing passwords
- **Social Engineering** - Tricking people, not computers
